How to Count Words in a Text

Word count is a simple measurement of how many words appear in a piece of writing. It is commonly used for essays, articles, assignments, applications, reports, and social content.

Why word count matters

Many writing tasks have minimum or maximum length requirements. A quick word count helps you check whether your draft fits the requested range without manually counting every word.

How online word counters work

A browser-based counter reads the text you enter and separates it into words using spaces and other text boundaries. The exact handling of punctuation can vary between tools.

Count words instantly

Paste your text into the free Word Counter. It updates the count as you type, so you can monitor your draft while editing.

Word count tips

  • Paste the complete draft for a final count.
  • Check the requirements for whether headings and references are included.
  • Do not add unnecessary words just to reach a target.
  • Use the counter while editing to keep long sections under control.

Word count and character count are different

Words are separated units of text, while characters include letters, numbers, spaces, and punctuation. A character counter is useful when a platform sets a character limit.
