Average vs Median: Which Should You Use?

average calculator, median calculator

The basic idea

The arithmetic mean is the sum of values divided by the number of values. The median is the middle value after sorting the values; when there are an even number of values, it is the average of the two middle values.

Example and common mistakes

For values 10, 11, 12, 13, and 100, the mean is 29.2 while the median is 12. The unusually large value pulls the mean upward. Median is often helpful for skewed distributions such as home prices, while the mean is useful when every value contributes to a total.
