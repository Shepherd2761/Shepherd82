Compound Interest: Formula and Examples

compound interest calculator, compound growth calculator

The basic idea

Compound interest means interest is earned on both the original balance and previously accumulated interest. With a fixed annual rate compounded once per year, the basic formula is principal multiplied by one plus the rate, raised to the number of years.

Example and common mistakes

A $1,000 balance growing at 5% annually becomes about $1,276.28 after five years before fees, taxes, or contributions. Real accounts may compound at different intervals and have variable rates, so a simple estimate may differ from a bank statement.
