How to Calculate BMI from Height and Weight

BMI calculator, body mass index formula

The basic idea

Body mass index is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in metres squared. It is a screening measure used in some adult health contexts, not a direct measurement of body fat or an individual diagnosis.

Example and common mistakes

For example, someone weighing 70 kg and measuring 1.75 m tall has a BMI of about 22.9. Interpretation can vary by age, pregnancy, muscle mass, and clinical context, and adult BMI categories are not intended to diagnose health by themselves.
