How to Calculate a Percentage

Percentages are used for discounts, grades, statistics, business calculations, and everyday comparisons. The basic idea is that a percentage expresses a number as a part of 100.

The basic percentage formula

To find a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100.

Formula: result = number × percentage ÷ 100

Example

To calculate 15% of 200, multiply 200 by 15 and divide by 100. The result is 30.

Where percentages are useful

  • Calculating discounts
  • Comparing changes in prices
  • Checking test scores
  • Understanding business metrics
  • Working with statistics

Percentage versus percentage points

A percentage describes a relative amount, while a percentage point describes the direct difference between two percentages. For example, moving from 20% to 25% is a 5 percentage-point increase.

Frequently asked questions

What is 10% of a number?

Divide the number by 10. For example, 10% of 80 is 8.

Can percentages be greater than 100?

Yes. A percentage above 100% means the amount is greater than the reference amount.
