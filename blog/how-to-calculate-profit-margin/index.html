How to Calculate Profit Margin

profit margin calculator, gross margin formula

The basic idea

Profit margin shows the share of revenue remaining after a particular cost measure. Gross profit is revenue minus cost of goods sold. Divide profit by revenue and multiply by 100 to express the margin as a percentage.

Example and common mistakes

If revenue is $200 and the relevant costs are $120, profit is $80 and the margin is 40%. Margin and markup are different: markup divides profit by cost, while margin divides profit by selling revenue. Confusing the two can lead to underpricing.
