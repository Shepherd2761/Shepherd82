How to Calculate a Tip and Split a Bill

tip calculator, split bill calculator

The basic idea

A tip calculation starts with the pre-tax bill in places where tipping is based on the meal subtotal. Multiply the bill by the tip percentage, then add the tip to the original amount. If several people share the bill equally, divide the total by the number of diners.

Example and common mistakes

For example, a $48 bill with an 18% tip creates an $8.64 tip and a $56.64 total. Four people splitting it evenly would each pay $14.16. These calculations are useful when a restaurant receipt does not show suggested tip amounts or when a group wants to divide costs fairly.
