How to Find the Number of Days Between Two Dates

Finding the number of days between two calendar dates is useful for project planning, deadlines, travel, subscriptions, age-related calculations, and many other tasks.

What does “date difference” mean?

Date difference is the elapsed time between a start date and an end date. The result can be expressed in days and then converted into approximate weeks or months.

Why manual calculation can be difficult

Months do not all contain the same number of days, and leap years add an extra day. A date calculator can account for these calendar differences automatically.

Example

If an event starts on March 1 and ends on March 11 of the same year, the elapsed difference is 10 days when counting the time between the two dates.

Important counting detail

There is a difference between elapsed days and inclusive counting. If you count both the starting and ending calendar dates as full included days, your total will be one higher than the elapsed difference.
