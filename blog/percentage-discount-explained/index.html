How to Calculate a Discount and Sale Price

discount calculator, percentage off calculator

The basic idea

To calculate a discount, multiply the original price by the discount percentage divided by 100. Subtract that saving from the original price to get the sale price. This method works for shopping, invoices, subscriptions, and price comparisons.

Example and common mistakes

A $80 item discounted by 25% saves $20, making the sale price $60 before any additional tax or fees. A discount is not the same as a final price: compare the actual amount payable, shipping, and other charges when shopping.
