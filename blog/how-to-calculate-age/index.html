How to Calculate Your Age Online

Calculating age sounds simple, but getting the exact number of years, months, and days can be surprisingly tricky because months have different lengths and leap years affect the result. An online age calculator can handle those date rules for you.

What do you need to calculate age?

You normally need two dates: your date of birth and the date on which you want to know your age. If you want your current age, the second date is simply today.

How age is calculated

The calculator compares the year, month, and day of the two dates. It first finds complete years, then the remaining months and days. This is more precise than simply dividing the number of days by 365.

Why the exact result can vary

A year is not always 365 days, and months range from 28 to 31 days. Leap years add another day to February. For this reason, calendar-based age calculations are preferable when you need an exact birthday-style result.

Quick example

If someone was born on January 15, 2000 and the calculation date is January 15, 2026, the calendar age is exactly 26 years. If the calculation date is a few days before January 15, the completed-year count will still be 25.

Can I calculate age for a past date?

Yes. Enter the past date in the calculation-date field.

Does a leap year matter?

Yes. Calendar dates around February 29 can require special handling, which is why a date-aware calculator is useful.
